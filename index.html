Services

What Ho Digital
does for you.

Four focused services, tailored for local businesses that want to look great and get found online.

01
Website Design & Development
Primary Service

Your website is your most powerful business tool — and for most small businesses, it's the difference between a customer calling you or your competitor. We build clean, fast, mobile-friendly sites tailored to your industry. Cafés, construction companies, salons, restaurants — if you have a business, we'll build you a site that makes it look the part.

  • → Custom design — no templates
  • → Mobile responsive on every device
  • → Contact forms & booking integrations
  • → Fast load speeds
  • → Built to convert visitors into customers
02
Logo & Brand Identity

First impressions matter. A strong logo and consistent brand identity tells customers you're professional before you say a single word. We create logos, color palettes, and visual systems that work everywhere — your website, your signage, your business cards, and your social media.

  • → Logo design
  • → Color palette & font selection
  • → Brand guidelines document
  • → Files ready for print & digital use
03
Google Business Profile Setup

When someone searches "café near me" or "plumber in [city]" — you need to show up. We fully set up and optimize your Google Business Profile so your business appears in local search results and on Google Maps with the right photos, hours, and contact info.

  • → Full profile setup & verification
  • → Photo uploads & description optimization
  • → Show up on Google Maps searches
  • → Hours, categories & services configured
04
Social Media Setup

Your Instagram and Facebook pages are often the first place a potential customer checks before they ever visit your website. We set up and design your social profiles to look polished, on-brand, and ready to post — so you make the right impression from day one.

  • → Instagram & Facebook setup
  • → Profile photo & cover image design
  • → Bio & branding aligned to your identity
  • → Ready to start posting immediately

About

Wilson
Ho.

I'm Wilson Ho — founder of Ho Digital and Ho Design Co., and the person behind every single project we take on. I started this company because I kept noticing the same thing: great local businesses losing customers every day because their website was outdated, broken, or didn't exist at all. That's a fixable problem, and fixing it is exactly what I do.

I design and build websites, brand identities, and digital setups for small businesses across the Pacific Northwest and beyond. Every project gets my full attention — no outsourcing, no junior team members. Just me, making sure your business looks exactly as good online as it does in person.

I work with all kinds of businesses — cafés, construction companies, salons, restaurants, retail shops. If you have a business and a vision, I'll make it visible. The goal is always the same: a digital presence that actually works for you, built fast and without the agency price tag.

Based in Bellevue and Tacoma, Washington. Serving businesses everywhere.
